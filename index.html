When the measurement is the hard part

Most ML projects show you a model. This one shows what happens when you take evaluation seriously enough to throw results away.

Accent Fairness in the 2026 ASR Generation

7 models · 3 inference runtimes · methodology frozen before any run
View repository →

An audit of seven production speech-recognition models for accent disparity — asking whether faster models are less fair, and whether the gap can be trained away. Every metric, exclusion rule and statistical test was written down and dated before any model produced a number, so a result could never quietly reshape the method that produced it.
